## Derivation checks

- **Monotonicity in τ_w,s.** At fixed m, dO/dτ_w = (K·m/2)·(1/√τ_w − 1/√r) ≤ 0, because 0 < r ≤ τ_w. At τ_w = τ/j the two sides agree (difference 0). m·R_s is a non-increasing step as τ_w,s grows. So Σ_s is non-increasing in τ_w,s and f is non-decreasing in τ_w,s (rv02). This is the plan's Q14 argument applied to side s. For the §9.3 Bounds corners: the pessimistic corner takes τ_w,s at its maximum and R_s at its minimum.
- **Boundary convention.** At τ = 3·τ_w,s exactly, m_s = 2 (rv02 (b)): the unit is not replaced, which is consistent with EQ-LIFECYCLE-REPLACEMENT-COUNT.
- **Renewal and finite horizon.** Covered in rv03 (a), (b) and (e). In the mixed case with strong √a ageing, the optimum is n = 1 (rv03 (b)), consistent with the sign of the left side of the condition.
- **Generalised inflection test.** sign f″ = sign(K_s²·α³ − K_g²·β³), with α = τ − a and β = τ − b. The ratio α/β is strictly monotone for a ≠ b, so D changes sign at most once on a segment, and an interior inflection exists iff D changes sign between the ends. Both end ages are differences of integer multiples of τ_w,g and τ_w,s, so the test can be made exact with rationals. In the old case (b = 0, a = m·τ_w), D(L⁺) = −K_g²·m³·τ_w³ < 0 and D(U⁻) > 0 iff K_s² > K_g²·(m+1)³ (rv04 (b)).
- **Segment root count.** Exact counting on pieces proven monotone (bisection of f′ either side of the analytic inflection, then sign changes of f between the zeros of f′) gave at most 3 roots over 1,500 random segments, and 3 roots with a < b (rv04 (c)–(d)). The grid was used only to confirm the example.
- **R_s accounting.** See rv06 (b): each flow is counted once under the finding-6 reading.
