## Inputs to recheck

Plan inputs (section 3.0 and 3.2) whose value disagrees with its source, or could not be found in it. "Disagrees" = a source was read and shows a different number. "Not seen" = source exists but the value was not visible to me (paywall, figure only, or blocked host).

### Disagrees with the source that was read

1. **AlSi10Mg conductivity by heat treatment (B2.9, R27).** Current EOS M 290 30 um product data sheet: as manufactured 100 (vertical) and 110 (horizontal), heat treated T6 165 and 155, stress-relieved 160 and 165 W/m/K. The plan's 103 (in plane), 119 (build), 173 (heat treated) are not in this edition; the older sheet was not seen. Directions are also reversed in sense: plan has build direction higher (119 > 103), EOS now has vertical (build) lower (100 < 110). Independent open data (Selo thesis 2022, which underlies R29): as-built 130 +/- 10 (build) and 115 +/- 9 (transverse), T6-like 165 +/- 14 and 166 +/- 14 W/m/K. The plan's ranges (as-built 100-130, treated 150-180) cover all of these, and the falsifier "as-built above 140" is not met. The k_Al set {100, 120, 150, 180, 200} is consistent; 200 is wrought-reference only.
2. **Pt/C exchange current density in acid (C02 P1: 0.2 A/cm2 at 298.15 K, range 0.1-1; R55).** Durst et al. 2015 Table II: 0.135 +/- 0.04 A/cm2_Pt at 313 K, 0.26 +/- 0.03 at 353 K; quoted literature at 293-298 K: 0.06-0.07 +/- 0.02. So 0.2 is about 3x high for 298 K and right only near 353 K. The range 0.1-1 does not include the 298 K values. alpha 0.47-0.53 supports alpha = 0.5.
3. **NiFe oxygen-evolution overpotentials at high current (C05 outside range "0.27-0.45 V at 0.5-1 A/cm2", R64).** Lu and Zhao 2015: 0.24 V at 0.5 A/cm2 and 0.27 V at 1 A/cm2 in 10 M KOH (Tafel-plot values); 0.27 V at 0.1 and 0.34 V at 0.3 A/cm2 in 30 wt% KOH; Tafel slope 32 mV/dec (inside the plan's 30-60). Plan's hand NiFe result 0.31-0.47 V at 1 A/cm2 is higher than the 0.27 V reported. Also the paper's roughness factor is 50 (capacitance-based, itself questionable per C2.10).
4. **Cumulative energy demand (B20, C13, R79).** Seen: Cu 53.7 total mix (59.3 primary from Mo route), Al 131 total mix (194 primary), Pt 243 000 total mix (308 000 primary ZA), Ir 169 000 MJ/kg. Plan's Cu 54, Al 131, Pt 2.4e5 are the production-mix totals, but the plan text calls Al "primary": for primary Al use 194. Per volume (derived): Al/Cu = 0.73 with totals, 0.98 with primary values; both inside the plan's 0.4-2 band. The Pt value 1.0e5 in C13 is not in the source (range there should be about 1.2e4 secondary to 3.3e5 primary). e_Ir not in plan; 1.65-1.75e5.
5. **Plan's PF5 length scale (R07, R08).** The cited Muzychka-Yovanovich model is built on sqrt(A), whereas PF5 uses D_h with the circular-duct 3.44. Not a numeric disagreement with a source, but the constants are used with a different length scale than the source's.
